Who We Are

A dedicated 3D imaging lab, not a general clinic.

3D LAB focuses on one thing: accurate, low-radiation 3D dental diagnostics. We work directly with patients and partner with dental clinics who need fast, reliable CBCT and X-ray imaging without sending patients across town. Every scan is captured on modern equipment and delivered as a clean digital file, ready for treatment planning the same day.

What We Offer

3D & digital imaging services

Every scan is captured, reviewed and exported digitally — ready to bring to your dentist or orthodontist the same day.

CBCT 3D Scan

Full jaw cone-beam 3D imaging for implant planning, surgery guidance and complex diagnostics.

Panoramic X-Ray (OPG)

A full-mouth overview in a single image — ideal for general checkups and treatment planning.

Cephalometric X-Ray

Skull-profile imaging used by orthodontists to plan braces and jaw alignment treatment.

Single Tooth X-Ray

Targeted periapical imaging for a fast look at one tooth or a specific problem area.

Digital Diagnostic Report

Clear written notes alongside your images, so you and your dentist know exactly what to look at.
